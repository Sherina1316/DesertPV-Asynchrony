# v24.6 methods and logic

## Reference-year endpoint
Every materially intersected 2024 SHP pixel is retained in the final 2024 active and cumulative products. The SHP is not rendered as a separate map overlay.

## Historical reconstruction
Standard temporal breaks are supplemented by an anchor-internal object trajectory route based on annual calibrated probability, LSMA PV fraction and confuser evidence. The inferred onset must itself satisfy the evidence gate, preventing an earlier rolling-window start from being reported as the installation year.

## Spatial topology
Candidates are evaluated as connected objects. Small expansion fronts are retained when connected to an earlier accepted footprint. Near-anchor and remote independent sites use progressively stricter size, compactness, probability, LSMA and confuser gates.

## Annual states
Cumulative installation is persistent. Annual activity is evidence-driven; the 2024 anchor is forced only in 2024, not copied into 2025.
